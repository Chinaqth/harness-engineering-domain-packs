# Domain Auto-Activation Generator-Evaluator Contract

## Generator

- Preserve draft registration and automatic content evaluation.
- Remove lifecycle-only human gates without weakening task permissions.
- Implement atomic registry-manifest activation and negative-path tests.

## Evaluator

- Remain read-only and independent.
- Reproduce draft registration, successful automatic activation, incomplete-Pack rejection,
  synchronized lifecycle state, and non-grant of operational authority.
- Return Pass, Fail, or Blocked with P0/P1 findings reported first.

## Evidence

Record exact commands, expected and actual outcomes, repository state, limitations, and rollback.
