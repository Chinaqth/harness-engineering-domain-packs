# Decision: Complete Engineering Web as a Public Baseline

## Decision

Build `engineering.web` as a reusable public professional baseline derived from authoritative
sources. Keep organization-specific reviewers, permissions, private architecture, commands, and
approval evidence as explicit activation gaps.

## Rationale

This preserves the boundary between durable professional practice and project or organization
policy, while allowing content completeness to be evaluated independently of activation authority.

## Lifecycle

The Domain remains `draft`. Activation requires a separate decision by the named owner and required
reviewers after organization-specific gaps are resolved.

## Outcome

Independent evaluation classified the Pack as `content_state=content-complete` and
`state=needs-org-input`. This satisfies the public-baseline decision while preserving the separate
activation authority boundary.

## Rollback

Restore the prior registered draft files under `domains/engineering/web/` and remove only
`changes/engineering.web-completion/`. Preserve the registry entry and all unrelated repository
changes.
