# Engineering Web Domain Completion Requirements

## Objective

Complete the registered draft Domain `engineering.web` from a current, authoritative public
professional baseline while preserving organization-specific unknowns and the draft lifecycle.

## Scope

- Research the Web frontend engineering profession from authoritative public and repository
  sources.
- Author and independently evaluate every declared production artifact.
- Validate routing, references, schemas, research traceability, and the assembled Pack.
- Report content completeness separately from activation readiness.

## Non-goals

- Do not invent reviewers, internal permissions, private architecture, product commands, or
  unpublished policy.
- Do not activate the Domain.
- Do not modify unrelated registered Domain Packs or pre-existing repository changes.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The research ledger and supporting outputs pass deterministic validation.
2. Every non-hidden production artifact is declared in `session.json` and has a current,
   independently produced passing evaluation with a score strictly greater than 90.
3. Registry, schema, reference, Skill, Agent, and repository checks pass.
4. Representative positive and negative routing scenarios are recorded.
5. A fresh independent Pack evaluator passes the complete directory.
6. Final evidence reports `content_state` and `state` without changing lifecycle status from
   `draft`.

## Risk

This is G2 work because it adds a complete routable professional capability surface and therefore
requires Generator-Evaluator separation and durable acceptance evidence.
