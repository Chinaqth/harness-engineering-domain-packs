# Generator–Evaluator Contract

The Builder persists research and evaluation envelopes and enforces order and budgets. Fresh Authors modify one artifact only. Fresh read-only Evaluators independently score the current digest. Passing requires score above 90, all hard gates, traceable ledger IDs, and no P0/P1 finding or blocked reason. The Pack remains draft unless all artifact, final, session, structural, routing, and repository gates pass.
