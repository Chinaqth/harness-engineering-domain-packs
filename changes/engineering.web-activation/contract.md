# Generator-Evaluator Contract

## Risk

G2 lifecycle activation and direct publication.

## Generator Responsibilities

- Preserve the professional baseline and Kernel red lines.
- Treat activation as routing eligibility, not an operational permission grant.
- Keep project-specific inputs fail-closed at the task contract or project overlay.
- Preserve unrelated working-tree changes.
- Provide reproducible activation, routing, permission, validation, and rollback evidence.

## Evaluator Responsibilities

- Remain read-only.
- Reproduce the activation-ready Pack verdict and active registry validation.
- Exercise a positive routing case and a negative missing-authority case.
- Confirm the approval evidence is scoped, durable, and does not expand permissions.
- Return exactly `Pass`, `Fail`, or `Blocked`; do not repair the change.

## Evidence Standard

Evidence must identify the evaluated revision or working-tree digest, environment, preconditions,
commands or journey, expected and actual results, durable paths, and limitations.
